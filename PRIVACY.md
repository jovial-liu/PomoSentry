# PomoSentry mobile privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026. Applies to the local-only iPhone and iPad edition.

## Data stays on your device

PomoSentry stores focus schedules, saved rule names, app/website selection tokens,
preferences and focus history locally. Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings
and Device Activity frameworks provide user-authorized Screen Time controls.
The app and its monitor extension share local data using an App Group. Selection
tokens are opaque system identifiers; PomoSentry does not send them to a developer
server. This edition has no enabled cross-device cloud synchronization.

PomoSentry does not collect data on a developer server, sell personal information,
use advertising or third-party analytics, or track users across apps or websites.
It does not upload website content or browsing history to the developer.

## Permissions and Apple services

Screen Time permission is requested for app and website protection. You may revoke
it in system settings. Notifications, if enabled by you, are used for reminders.
Apple manages these permissions, system restrictions and App Store distribution.
Device backups and Apple's system services are governed by your Apple settings
and Apple's policies; local-only storage is not a promise that device backups are
disabled. Restrictions depend on OS capabilities, and can be removed by revoking
permission or uninstalling. In-app schedule locking is not an OS security boundary.

## Exports and support

If you choose to export rules or share diagnostics, the recipient receives what
you explicitly share. Sharing is optional. GitHub support issues are public and
are processed by GitHub under its own privacy policy. Do not post selection-token
exports, private schedules, browsing history, device identifiers, passwords or
other personal information. Redact screenshots before posting.

## Retention and your choices

Saved data remains on the device until removed through available app controls,
app removal or device-data management. A locked schedule may restrict in-app
editing until its committed period ends. Revoking permission stops authorized
Screen Time controls but does not itself erase saved schedules. System backups
may retain copies even after app removal.

## Contact and changes

Product support and privacy questions: [PomoSentry support](https://github.com/jovial-liu/PomoSentry/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).
No private contact details are required in public support issues. Any future
change enabling cloud sync, analytics or server collection must be reflected in
this policy and the app's App Store privacy disclosures before that release.

# PomoSentry 移动版隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日。适用于本地保存的 iPhone、iPad 版本。

应用将预约、名单名称、应用与网站的系统选择标识、偏好及专注记录保存在设备上，
通过本机 App Group 与监控扩展共享。使用 Apple 屏幕使用时间框架执行用户授权的
限制；不会将这些选择标识、网页正文或浏览历史上传到开发者服务器。本版本未启用
多端云同步，不提供广告、第三方统计、跨应用追踪或个人数据出售。

屏幕使用时间权限用于应用和网站守护；通知权限在你开启提醒时使用。权限、系统
限制及 App Store 分发由 Apple 管理。设备备份和系统服务遵循你的 Apple 设置与
Apple 的政策；本地保存不代表系统备份被关闭。撤销权限或卸载可能解除限制；应用
中的预约锁定不等同于操作系统的安全边界。

导出名单或发送诊断由你主动选择，接收者将收到你明确分享的内容。GitHub 支持
问题是公开的，受 GitHub 的隐私政策约束。请勿公开名单标识、私人预约、浏览历史、
设备标识、密码或其他个人信息；截图应先遮盖个人信息。

数据保留至通过可用的应用功能、卸载或设备数据管理删除。已锁定预约可能在执行
期间禁止应用内编辑；撤销授权不会自动删除保存的预约。系统备份可能继续保留数据。

产品支持与隐私问题请使用上方支持入口。以后若启用云同步、统计或服务器数据
收集，应在相应版本发布前更新本政策及 App Store 隐私声明。

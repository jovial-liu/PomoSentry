# PomoSentry support

PomoSentry provides calendar-based focus schedules and saved app/website rules.
The mobile edition uses Apple's Screen Time permission. Schedule locking freezes
editing/cancellation; restrictions begin at the scheduled start, not at lock time.

## Get help

[Open a support issue](https://github.com/jovial-liu/PomoSentry/issues/new) or
[browse existing reports](https://github.com/jovial-liu/PomoSentry/issues).
GitHub issues are public: do not share passwords, selection-token exports,
private appointments, browsing history or device identifiers. A short description,
app version, OS version and redacted screenshot are usually sufficient.

For a missed restriction, say whether the app was already open at the scheduled
start or was reopened later. Authorization and a successful monitoring callback
do not prove every app or website was actually blocked. System selection limits,
background callbacks and browser support affect protection. No absolute
anti-bypass or hard real-time guarantee is made.

The device owner can revoke Screen Time access in system settings. This can remove
restrictions but does not delete saved schedules. Uninstalling can remove app
restrictions and local data; preserve needed data first. Do not delete the app
as an ordinary update step.

[Mobile privacy policy](https://github.com/jovial-liu/PomoSentry/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)
